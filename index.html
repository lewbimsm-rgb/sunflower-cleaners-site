Good to know

Questions we hear at the counter.

How long does dry cleaning take?

Most items are ready in 2–3 days. Delicate and specialty pieces can need a little more time. If you're in a genuine pinch, ask at the counter. When we can help sooner, we do.

Do you do alterations in-house?

Yes — hems, take-ins, repairs, and rescues are done right here in the shop, with every alteration quoted individually at the counter so you know the price before we start.

Can you clean and preserve wedding gowns?

Yes, wedding gown cleaning and preservation is one of our specialties. Bring the gown in and we'll walk you through the process and timing in person.

Do you clean leather, suede, and hats?

We do — leather and suede garments and hat cleaning are both specialty services here, ones that most cleaners in the valley no longer offer.

How do I pay?

Cash, all major cards, and tap to pay including Apple Pay. Payment is taken at drop-off, which keeps pickup quick.

Where exactly are you?

50 N Valle Verde Dr, Suite 120, in Henderson, in the shopping center in front of Kohl's, just off the 215. Free parking right at the door.
